# Limitations and Engineering Safety Report

## 1. Geometric & Computer Vision Limitations

- **Single-View 2D Projection Ambiguity**:
  - A single 2D image only contains information on two spatial axes ($X$ and $Y$). The third dimension ($Z$, extrusion depth or plate thickness) cannot be determined through computer vision alone.
  - The application logs an explicit uncertainty report and requires user input or a designated design parameter for thickness.
- **Hidden Geometry & Internal Voids**:
  - Internal cooling channels, undercut threads, or hidden blind cavities not visible in the 2D projection cannot be recovered from a single raster image without secondary views or section drawings.
- **Perspective Distortion**:
  - Perspective photos taken from off-axis camera angles require quadrilateral perspective rectification using reference landmarks before feature scaling. Orthographic scans and engineering drawings are recommended.

## 2. CNC Machining & G-Code Safety Notice

- **Safety Warning**:
  - G-code generated by the CAM module must be visually reviewed in a CNC simulator (e.g. CAMotics, NC Viewer, or LinuxCNC preview) before running on live industrial machine tools.
- **Workholding & Fixturing**:
  - The software assumes standard vise, fixture clamp, or vacuum table clearances above $Z=0$. Toolpaths must be checked to prevent toolholder collisions with physical clamps.
- **Speeds and Feeds**:
  - Default cutting parameters are calibrated for generic 6061-T6 Aluminum. Always verify feed per tooth, surface footage (SFM), and spindle horsepower for your specific tooling and raw material stock.
